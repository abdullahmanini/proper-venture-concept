# Proper: a venture concept

A FinTech venture concept by Abdullah Al Mneni, written for Diagram Ventures' Summer 2027 Venture Creation application. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Diagram.

Proper would show Canadian trade contractors when each invoice is legally due under prompt-payment rules, and which one protects the next payroll. Later, it could pay them early on invoices the law has already made payable.

- Live page: https://abdullahmanini.github.io/proper-venture-concept/
- `index.html`: the pitch page and clickable prototype. The contractor is fictional and all product data is synthetic.
- `Diagram_Venture_Thematic_Analysis.pdf`: the 2-page thesis memo, with sources.
- `Diagram_Market_Sizing.xlsx`: the bottom-up market model. All rates in it are labelled assumptions.

Nothing here has been validated with customers yet. The legal timelines shown are simplified Ontario rules for illustration and are not legal advice.
