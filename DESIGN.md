## Rules

- Flat surfaces only. No shadows and no gradients. Sections are separated by 1px hairlines and split into grid cells.
- Headings use Ubuntu at regular weight with tight tracking. Body text is Roboto.
- Orange marks the one thing that needs attention on a screen (the payroll date, the gap). Use it sparingly.
- Buttons are outlined black rectangles with an arrow, or solid black when primary.
- The hero uses large overlapping circles and quarter shapes in periwinkle, orange, oxblood and black.
- One dark band (#171717) per page at most.
- Every page carries the authorship banner. Never use Diagram's logo or wordmark.
